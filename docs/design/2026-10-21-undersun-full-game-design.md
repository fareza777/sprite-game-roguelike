# UNDERSUN — full game design (v1.0 package)

Supersedes the OATHDEEP slice design. The user asked (2026-10-21) for a complete, serious package and told us to
decide everything ourselves ("gak usah tanya2 lagi, gas"). Every creative call below is ours unless marked *user*.

## Intent
A premium, portrait, one-thumb-friendly pixel-art action roguelike for Android with a story people remember.
Success = a polished APK that feels like a finished commercial game: branded, narrated, ten distinct floors,
readable controls, deep but simple loot, and content variety (NPCs/events, not just fights).

User requirements (*user*): new unique name; best app icon; splash; cinematic intro with narration + VO;
onboarding; full menu with settings / about / share / rate; original story; 5 heroes with distinct stats + skills;
40 enemies incl. bosses with a codex unlocked on defeat; item, equipment, main-quest and side-quest pages;
100+ items with procedural random stats; NPCs, merchants and events in the dungeon; 10 floors, each a different
biome + design + boss, with large floors/rooms; good SFX; fix the joystick (sticks to the bottom-left corner) and
move the shield/skill button so it doesn't get in the way; push to github.com/fareza777/sprite-game-roguelike.

Anti-goals: placeholder art in shipped screens; a copy-paste story; controls that fight the thumb; any use of
the user's API keys file; paid/online services (everything is generated offline).

## Constraints (ours unless *user*)
- Unity 6000.3 URP 2D, Android portrait, IL2CPP ARM64 (*user*: Unity MCP for engine work).
- All art through the Aseprite MCP (*user*), authored with our Python generators + Apollo palette.
- All audio synthesized offline (`tools/audio`); VO = Windows SAPI voice (only "Microsoft Zira" is installed),
  processed into an ethereal narrator. This shapes the story: the narrator is the buried Sun, a female voice.
- In-game language: English (global Play Store audience). Package id `com.fareza777.undersun`.
- Single Unity scene; UI built in code (existing pattern); content generated by `Oathdeep > Build Everything`.

## Name & brand
**UNDERSUN** — no exact match found on web search (closest unrelated: UnderDungeon, Undungeon).
Tagline: *"The sun was buried. You are what it sings for."*
Palette: dusk navy + ember gold; logo = UNDERSUN in carved gold letters over a half-buried sun.

## Story (original)
Nine hundred years ago a Hunger crept across the sky and began eating the light. The ten voices of the
**Choir of Dawn** sang the Sun *down* into the earth to hide her, sealing her beneath ten vaults. Each Chorister
stayed below to hold one **Verse** of the sealing hymn. The world above fell into an endless dusk; the last city,
**Emberwick**, lives on rationed lantern-oil.

Now the lanterns are failing — and from the deep, a voice has begun to sing. It is **the Undersun** herself, calling
for the **Unsung**: those whose names were never written in any hymn. Descend, silence each Verse, raise the Sun.

The Choristers have held their verses alone for nine centuries. Each verse warped its vault into a biome and its
singer into the floor's boss. Lore in NPC dialogue and codex entries reveals the twist: the Hunger never came from
the sky — the Choir *was* the Hunger; they buried the Sun to keep her light for themselves. The final Chorister,
**the Cantor**, begs you to stop: "If she rises, she will remember who buried her." Ending: the Sun rises, dusk
breaks, and the narrator's last line hints at her own hunger (sequel / New Descent hook).

Narration: the Sun ("Sol" in the codex), ethereal female voice. Story beats delivered by: intro cinematic (6 panels),
floor-entry lines (10), boss intro barks (10), NPC dialogue, codex lore, ending cinematic (4 panels).

= 30 regular + 10 bosses = **40 codex entries**. Regular enemies reuse AI archetypes with biome-specific stats,
art and attack flavour: Chaser, Lunger, Ranged, Hopper/Splitter, Charger, Caster (homing orbs), Bomber, Teleporter,

Each floor: 16–20 rooms (bigger shapes than the slice), its own tileset, props, light colour, music and ambience.

## Rooms (not every room is a fight)
Combat · Elite (fewer, tougher, guaranteed item) · Merchant (Old Tallow, buy/sell) · Shrine (event with a choice) ·
Pilgrim/NPC (lore + side quests) · Rest (campfire: heal or upgrade) · Treasure · Boss. Per floor roughly
55% combat/elite, 45% other. Events: Shrine of Ash (HP for a relic), Wishing Well (gold for a blessing),
Cursed Chest (elite ambush for loot), Wounded Unsung (heal → reward later), Bone Dice (gamble gold),
Echo of a Chorister (lore + buff), Lost Lantern (side-quest item).

## Items (100+, procedural)
Runtime `ItemInstance` = base definition + rarity + rolled stats + affixes. Slots: Weapon, Armor, Charm, Relic.
- 64 base items (16 per slot) with stat ranges and a 16×16 icon each.
- 5 rarities (Common → Legendary) scale roll ranges and affix count (0–3).
- 24 affixes (prefix/suffix: of Embers = burn chance, Vampiric = lifesteal, Swift = move speed, Thorned, etc.).
- 40 named Legendaries/Uniques with fixed identity + unique effect (e.g. *Choirbreaker*, *Sol's Last Ember*).
→ 104 distinct item identities; every drop rolls fresh numbers scaled by floor depth.
Pages: Inventory/Equipment (4 slots + 16 backpack, compare, equip, sell), Item details.

## Quests
Main quest: Prologue + 10 chapters (one per Verse) + Epilogue, shown in the Quest page with lore.
Side quests: 12 authored (from NPCs and events), tracked across runs (persistent), e.g. "Bones for the Pilgrim",
"Light the Six Lanterns", "Silence 50 of the Unsung Dead", "A Merchant's Debt", "The Drowned Hymnal".
Rewards: gold, starting relics, codex lore.

## Meta & persistence
`SaveSystem` (JSON in persistentDataPath): settings, onboarding/intro flags, codex unlocks, quest state,
best depth, total kills, gold banked. Codex entries unlock on first kill (bosses show full lore).

## Front end
Splash (studio line + UNDERSUN logo, sunrise animation) → intro cinematic on first launch (skippable, replayable)
→ Main menu (animated dusk backdrop): Descend, Heroes, Codex, Quests, Settings, About, Share, Rate.
Settings: music/SFX sliders, vibration, screen shake, damage numbers, joystick mode (floating/fixed),
left-handed layout, replay intro, reset progress (confirm). About: credits, version, story blurb.
Share: Android ACTION_SEND with the Play Store link. Rate: `market://details?id=com.fareza777.undersun`
(fallback https). Onboarding: first run in the first room — move, attack, dodge, skill, loot, doors (skippable).

## Controls (fix)
The floating stick is crammed into the bottom-left corner; the skill button overlaps the play space.
New layout: stick zone = left 50% of the lower 45%, default resting base raised and inset (thumb-natural),
clamped so the base never sits within 70 units of a screen edge; right cluster in a thumb arc: Attack (large,
bottom-right), Dodge (left of attack), Skill (above-left of attack, away from the screen centre), all ≤35% screen
height. Optional fixed stick + left-handed mirror in Settings.

## Audio
Per-floor music (10 loops, each its own key/tempo/instrument set) + boss loop + menu/intro/ending cues; ~60 SFX
(per-archetype attacks, elements: fire/ice/water/poison/arcane/gears, UI, merchant, quest, level events);
VO lines for intro/ending/floor entries/boss intros.
